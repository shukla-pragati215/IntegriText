Help Center

Find answers to common questions

How does the Plagiarism Checker work?

Our plagiarism checker compares your text against billions of web pages and academic databases to identify matching content. Results show the percentage of original vs. matched content.

What is the AI Detector?

The AI Detector analyzes writing patterns, sentence structure, and linguistic markers to determine the probability that text was generated by an AI model like ChatGPT or similar tools.

How does the AI Humanizer work?

The AI Humanizer rewrites AI-generated text to sound more natural and human-like by varying sentence structure, adding colloquialisms, and adjusting tone while preserving the original meaning.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All text submissions are encrypted and processed securely. We do not store your content after analysis is complete. Your privacy is our top priority.

How many languages are supported for translation?

We support over 50 languages for translation including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and many more.
